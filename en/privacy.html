Privacy policy

Privacy policy for the New Vision Optics website.

Information you send

When you contact us through WhatsApp or email, information is sent through the service you selected. The website does not send patient reports, medical details, names, phone numbers, email addresses, WhatsApp message text, or form content to Analytics.

Cookies and analytics

With your permission, the website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and marketing interactions such as WhatsApp, appointment, phone, directions, shop, Instagram, file, video and page interactions. The Google tag is not loaded before consent. You may allow or decline analytics using the notice shown on the website.

Campaign attribution

Campaign parameters in links may be retained for the browsing session so marketing source, medium and campaign performance can be measured. Existing valid campaign attribution is not overwritten.

External links

The website links to WhatsApp, Instagram and NVO Shop. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.
